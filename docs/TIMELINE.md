# Phased application timeline - as of 2 October 2026

> **Version stamp.** As of **2 October 2026**, the phased application timeline of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 stands as follows. **This is subject to ongoing legislative amendment.** I would re-verify against EUR-Lex before any operational use.

## What this is based on

- **Base text:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of 13 June 2024, as published in the Official Journal on 12 July 2024 (the PDF supplied with this project). Article numbers in the crosswalk refer to that text.
- **Amending act:** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the "Digital Omnibus on AI"). On 2 Oct 2026 I tried to read the Official Journal text through a fetch tool. It returned the **recitals and the early amending articles only**: the text was cut off around the amended Article 57, so **the operative amendment of Article 113 (the dates article) was not reached**. The tool also summarises rather than copies, so quotations it returned are not guaranteed verbatim. What I could and could not confirm is itemised in the table at the end.
- **No consolidated version** of 2024/1689 incorporating 2026/1744 was used.

## Are the high-risk dates fixed or conditional? (checked 2 Oct 2026)

**Reading: fixed calendar dates, not standards-conditional.** Evidence, in order of weight:

1. **Recital 40 of the amending regulation** (read in the OJ text): the dates of application of Chapter III Sections 1-3 are "set to" 2 December 2027 (Art. 6(2) / Annex III) and 2 August 2028 (Art. 6(1) / Annex I). The same recital asks the Commission to ensure support measures (guidance, standards, common specifications, codes of practice) are in place in due time. That is an instruction to the Commission, not a condition that suspends the dates.
2. **Gibson Dunn** (describing the trilogue agreement): the agreed text "replaces the Commission's originally proposed conditional trigger mechanism with these fixed dates". **White & Case** (after entry into force) calls it "a fixed timeline" with no standards-availability qualification.
3. **Where the word "conditional" comes from.** The Commission's original proposal used a conditional mechanism (per Gibson Dunn); sources written about the proposal, or about the Art. 111(2) rule, can read as "conditional". One law-firm note describes the dates as conditional on **no substantial change to systems already on the market**. That is the Art. 111(2) transitional rule (legacy systems are caught only if significantly changed in design), not a standards trigger. I did not find a post-adoption source that makes the dates standards-conditional, but I could not read the operative Article 113 text, so this remains **"strongly indicated, not primary-verified"**.

**What you should still do:** open the Official Journal text (EUR-Lex, "Regulation (EU) 2026/1744") and read the point that replaces Article 113 of 2024/1689. Confirm it states the two dates without a Commission-decision condition.

## Notes on specific amendments

- **Article 4 (AI literacy) was amended.** Several sources and the OJ text I could read agree the duty moved from ensuring a sufficient level to taking measures to support the development of AI literacy. The crosswalk's Art. 4 rows (`PRV-ALL-A04-LIT`, `DEP-ALL-A04-LIT`) already carry this status. The exact wording differs slightly between sources (one retains "a sufficient level of"), so I paraphrase and do not quote it.
- **New Art. 5(1) prohibitions.** Gibson Dunn says the new prohibition is subject to a transitional period until 2 Dec 2026. One OJ-text fetch read recital 46 as meaning the prohibitions apply from entry into force, but that fetch did not reach Article 113 either, so I treat the **2 Dec 2026** date as secondary-sourced and unresolved. Art. 5 is out of scope for this crosswalk, so no row depends on it.

## Legislative path of the amending act (secondary sources agree)

Political agreement 7 May 2026 -> Parliament vote 16 Jun 2026 -> Council approval 29 Jun 2026 -> OJ publication 24 Jul 2026 -> entry into force 27 Jul 2026 (third day after publication).

## Other substantive changes recorded in the crosswalk (all via secondary sources)

- **Art. 4 (AI literacy):** from "ensure ... a sufficient level" to "take measures to support the development of" AI literacy.
- **Art. 10(5) deleted; new Art. 4a** on processing special-category data for bias detection and correction.
- **Art. 11 / 17 / 63:** simplified technical documentation and proportionate QMS extended from SMEs to small mid-caps (SMCs).
- **Art. 27(4):** deployers may cross-reference existing DPIAs for the fundamental-rights impact assessment.
- **Art. 42(3) (new):** high-risk systems complying with the Cyber Resilience Act's cybersecurity requirements are deemed to comply with Art. 15 cybersecurity.
- **Art. 43(3):** revised for sectoral notified bodies.
- **Art. 57:** national sandbox deadline moved; Union-level sandbox by the AI Office.
- **Art. 74/75:** AI Office exclusive competence over AI systems built on a provider's own GPAI model and over systems embedded in very large online platforms/search engines.
