# Phased application timeline - as of 2 October 2026

> **Version stamp.** As of **2 October 2026**, the phased application timeline of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 stands as follows. **This is subject to ongoing legislative amendment.** I would re-verify against EUR-Lex before any operational use.

## What this is based on

- **Base text:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of 13 June 2024, as published in the Official Journal on 12 July 2024 (the PDF supplied with this project). Article numbers in the crosswalk refer to that text.
- **Amending act:** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the "Digital Omnibus on AI"). It was **not** the text supplied; it was researched from secondary sources (see below). **A consolidated version of 2024/1689 incorporating 2026/1744 was not used.**

## Legislative path of the amending act (secondary sources agree)

Political agreement 7 May 2026 -> Parliament vote 16 Jun 2026 -> Council approval 29 Jun 2026 -> OJ publication 24 Jul 2026 -> entry into force 27 Jul 2026 (third day after publication).

## Other substantive changes recorded in the crosswalk (all via secondary sources)

- **Art. 4 (AI literacy):** from "ensure ... a sufficient level" to "take measures to support the development of" AI literacy.
- **Art. 10(5) deleted; new Art. 4a** on processing special-category data for bias detection and correction.
- **Art. 11 / 17 / 63:** simplified technical documentation and proportionate QMS extended from SMEs to small mid-caps (SMCs).
- **Art. 27(4):** deployers may cross-reference existing DPIAs for the fundamental-rights impact assessment.
- **Art. 42(3) (new):** high-risk systems complying with the Cyber Resilience Act's cybersecurity requirements are deemed to comply with Art. 15 cybersecurity.
- **Art. 43(3):** revised for sectoral notified bodies.
- **Art. 57:** national sandbox deadline moved; Union-level sandbox by the AI Office.
- **Art. 74/75:** AI Office exclusive competence over AI systems built on a provider's own GPAI model and over systems embedded in very large online platforms/search engines.
